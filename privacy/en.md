# Bodr privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026.

Bodr does not collect, share or sell your data. The app has no servers, accounts, ads or analytics, and it makes no network connections.

**What Bodr reads.** With your permission, Bodr only reads from the Health app: heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and heartbeat series, respiratory rate, VO2 max, sleep, workouts and their distance, steps, active and resting energy, exercise time, irregular rhythm, high and low heart rate notifications, and your date of birth and sex. Bodr writes nothing to Health.

**What Bodr stores.** Its results, your journal answers and your settings stay on this iPhone, in the app's storage and in a folder the app shares with its widget. A copy of the data it read is kept in the app's cache so it opens faster. Like any app data on iPhone, it is part of your iPhone backup if you make one; Bodr itself sends it nowhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

**Notifications.** Reminders and summaries are created on the phone itself, without a server.

**Children.** Bodr is not meant for children and collects no data about them.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version appears at this address with a new date.
